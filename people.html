PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Seung-Won Kim 김승원

Assistant Professor
Department of Intelligent Robotics
School of Mechanical System Engineering
Seoul National University of Science and Technology

Prof. Kim leads the EIR Lab. at Seoul National University of Science and Technology. His research focuses on soft robotic mechanisms based on smart materials and structures, bio-inspired robots, wearable devices for human augmentation, and service robots designed for operation in human-centered environments.
