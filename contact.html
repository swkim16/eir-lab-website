WHO

We welcome motivated individuals interested in advancing the field of soft robotics and developing innovative robotic systems. If you are interested in our research and would like to contribute to the development of innovative robotic technologies, we encourage you to get in touch with us.

Currently, our research focuses on:

  • (1) robotic fruit harvesting
  • (2) robotic tools and mechanisms for healthcare applications
  • (3) variable-stiffness mechanisms
  • (4) soft wearable robots
  • (5) bio-inspired robots
